A little help with the maths.

Once you have a buy and sell price, your realised profit and commission are calculated for you.

Profit shareA percentage of positive profit. Losing trades carry no commission under this rule.
Turnover feeA percentage of the buy and sell values combined, multiplied by quantity.
Flat feeA fixed commission for each closed trade, regardless of the result.
