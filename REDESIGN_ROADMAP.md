# Alder & Vane redesign roadmap

**Alder & Vane** is the public name of the existing Flask/Jinja app at `ordervain.vercel.app`. Aim for the feel of a private investment office. An advisor opening the dashboard should immediately see commission earned. A client opening the portal should immediately see what their portfolio is worth and how to record a transaction.

## Design decisions

- Use a warm paper canvas `#F8F6F0`, ink `#202529`, and secondary text `#565F62`. Give the wordmark, active navigation and primary action a deep aubergine `#542D49`. Use white on filled aubergine buttons. Reserve moss `#236146` and oxblood `#913E42` for signed gains, losses and clearly labelled status. Pale stone `#DCD8CF` is a decorative divider. Give input boundaries and keyboard focus stronger contrast.
- Set **Alder & Vane** in Georgia or the local system serif, with a simple **AV** mark for small spaces. Use the bundled DM Sans for controls, tables and figures. Use tabular numerals, right-align comparable amounts, and preserve ₹, precision, dates and the app’s existing financial labels.
- Build hierarchy with alignment and space. Start with 32–48 px between sections on desktop and 24–32 px on phones. Give ledger rows a single bottom rule. Place ordinary summaries and charts directly on the canvas. Reserve enclosed surfaces and shadows for interactions that need a boundary, such as a dialog, menu or drop zone. Use one filled primary action per action group.
- Use direct headings such as “Overview” and “Client statements”. Remove taglines and decorative eyebrows. Keep time ranges, price timestamps, validation messages, charge explanations and anything else a user needs to interpret a number or complete an action.
